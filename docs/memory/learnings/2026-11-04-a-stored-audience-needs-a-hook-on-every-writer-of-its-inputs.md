## 2026-11-04 — architecture — a resolution stored at write time needs a hook on every writer of its inputs
**Context**: trinity-enterprise#1095 (and #961, #1096 — the same class, three triggers). An ask's audience is resolved once at raise and stored (`operator_queue.resolved_to` / `addressed_to_email`); every ending door reads the stored list. The assignments router wrote a new primary with no hook into pending rows, so a seat change left an approval answerable only by the person who no longer held the seat — and, with no primary at raise time, by nobody even after one was seated.
**Lesson**: When a value is a snapshot of a resolution (audience, approver, owner), census the WRITERS of the resolution's inputs and give each one a re-resolve call — `ask_service.readdress_role` / `readdress_item` are the primitive, called fail-soft after the write. Keep the selector an ALLOWLIST of the families addressed by the role (the agent's raises, `gate-` + 40 hex approvals); a family addressed to a named person defaults the same `to_role` and must not move. Writers found by the census that still lack the hook (`um_service.suspend_user`, `member_agents/removal.py`, the unshare of #961) are debt to register, not to forget.
