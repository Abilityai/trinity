## 2026-10-20 — pitfall — A session-revocation check must cover every `jwt.decode` of a platform token, and the voice socket is one
**Context**: #3825. Adding the per-user session cutoff, the plan named the three readers #187's logout revoke lives in (`resolve_platform_user_unfloored`, `decode_token` for `/ws`, `/api/auth/validate`). `grep -rn "jwt.decode(" src/backend` found a fourth: `routers/voice.py::voice_websocket` decodes the query-param token itself and had never checked the #187 `jti` blacklist either, so a logged-out (and now a password-changed) token still opened a voice session.
**Lesson**: When adding a check that must hold for every platform session, enumerate the doors by `grep -rn "jwt.decode(" src/backend`, not by memory or by the list a previous fix used. Separate the portal/MFA decoders (they gate on `scope`) from the platform-session readers, and put the new check in every one of the latter, with a test that drives the door to its first post-gate step.
