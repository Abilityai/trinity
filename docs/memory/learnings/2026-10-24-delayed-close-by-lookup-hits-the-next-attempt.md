## 2026-10-24 — pitfall — A close that looks up "the open activity" after a re-queue can close the next attempt's bar
**Context**: trinity#3932. The lease reaper commits `requeue_expired_lease` (row back to `queued`), then awaits `_close_requeued_activity`, which found the activity through `get_open_activity_id_for_execution` (started first, newest). A worker on another uvicorn worker, or the same one at an await, can re-claim the row in that gap and open the new attempt's bar. The delayed lookup then CANCELLED the live bar, and the later SUCCESS close found nothing, because CANCELLED is never upgraded.
**Lesson**: When a state write makes a new opener possible, read the id of the activity you mean to close BEFORE that write commits, and close it with `activity_id=`. An execution-id lookup is only safe while nothing else can open an activity for that execution. The reaper does this through `LeaseReapReport.requeued_activity_ids`, and a re-delivered claim always opens its own bar and never reuses an open one.
