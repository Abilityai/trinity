## 2026-09-24 — pitfall — Content placed ABOVE a chat pane's empty state is scrolled out of view by stick-to-bottom
**Context**: trinity-enterprise#465. The empty-chat suggestions were mounted in `PortalConversation`'s `#empty` slot above `PortalBriefing`; the transcript scroller sticks to the bottom, so on a normal viewport the new list sat above the fold, cut off at the top edge, with only its third item partly visible. Every unit and mounted test passed — jsdom has no layout — and the defect was found only by the live screenshot pass.
**Lesson**: anything added to the empty-chat state goes BETWEEN the agent's identity and its hints (`PortalBriefing`'s `before-hints` slot), sized to fit one viewport with the hints — never prepended to the slot. For any new Workspace surface inside the transcript scroller, the screenshot at a real viewport is the test; plan it, don't treat it as polish.
